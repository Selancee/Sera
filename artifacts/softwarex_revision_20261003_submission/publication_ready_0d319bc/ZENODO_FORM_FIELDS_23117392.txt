Title
SeraEdit: Scoped and Validated Language-Guided MusicXML Editing (SoftwareX R1 source and evidence)

Version
softwarex-r1-20261003

Description
SeraEdit is local-first research software for scoped, source-bound MusicXML editing through ScorePatch transactions, deterministic validation and reversible execution. This SoftwareX R1 source and evidence revision preserves the original v1.0.0 deposit while correcting fixture reproducibility and source-archive completeness. It includes saved DeepSeek three-condition outputs, a 200-case constructed-error study, and native MuseScore bridge screenshots with XML verification.

On the development benchmark, strict editing success is 72/110 for Full Rewrite, 65/110 for Patch Only and 63/110 for Sera Full. Sera Full records no measured non-target changes among 73 output scores, but erroneously refuses 34 executable requests. These results demonstrate a protection/completion tradeoff; they do not establish overall superiority or held-out generalization. The 720-run local rule-based replay is regression evidence on the same benchmark.
