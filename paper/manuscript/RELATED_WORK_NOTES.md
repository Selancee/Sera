# Related-work verification notes

Checked on 2026-08-05. These notes record source boundaries; they are not a substitute for a final literature review.

- **MusicXML 4.0:** W3C Music Notation Community Group Final Report, 1 June 2021. It describes MusicXML as an open digital-sheet-music exchange/archive format and explicitly notes that the report is not a W3C standard-track document: https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/
- **music21:** Cuthbert and Ariza, ISMIR 2010, pp. 637–642: https://ismir2010.ismir.net/proceedings/ismir2010-108.pdf
- **ChatMusician:** symbolic music understanding/generation using ABC as a text-compatible representation; Findings of ACL 2024: https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.373/
- **Can LLMs “Reason” in Music?:** ISMIR 2024; reports limitations in advanced, multi-step symbolic music reasoning: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21531
- **Not that Groove:** a recent editing-oriented preprint on zero-shot instruction-driven MIDI drum editing and programmatic tests. It is not a MusicXML/staff-notation system: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.08203
- **CodeEditorBench:** used only as a cross-domain motivation for testing editing separately from generation: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03543
